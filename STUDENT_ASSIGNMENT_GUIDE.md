# 📚 Student Assignment Submission Guide

## Overview
This guide explains how students can view their assignments and submit files through the LMS application.

---

## 🎯 How to Access Assignments

### Method 1: From Course Details Page
1. Navigate to **My Courses** from the dashboard or navigation menu
2. Click on any enrolled course
3. Click the **"View Assignments"** button (purple/accent colored button)
4. You'll be taken to the assignments page for that course

### Method 2: From Student Dashboard
1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. In the "My Enrolled Courses" section, find the course
3. Click the **assignment icon** button (📄 icon) next to "Continue"
4. You'll be taken directly to the assignments page

### Method 3: Direct Navigation
- Click on **"My Submissions"** in the navigation menu to see all your submissions across all courses

---

## 📝 Viewing Assignments

On the assignments page, you'll see:

### Assignment Card Information:
- **Title & Description**: What the assignment is about
- **Due Date**: When the assignment is due
- **Status Badge**: 
  - 🟡 **Not Submitted** - Assignment pending
  - 🔴 **Overdue** - Past the due date
  - 🔵 **Submitted - Pending Review** - Submitted but not graded
  - 🟢 **Graded: XX/100** - Graded with score

---

## 📤 How to Submit an Assignment

### Step 1: Choose Submission Method
You have **THREE options** for submitting:
1. **Text Response Only** - Type your answer
2. **File Upload Only** - Upload a document/file
3. **Both Text and File** - Combination of both

⚠️ **Note**: At least one option is required (either text or file)

### Step 2: Text Submission (Optional)
1. Find the **"Text Response"** textarea
2. Type your assignment submission
3. You can write essays, answers, explanations, etc.

### Step 3: File Upload (Optional)
1. Find the **"Upload File"** section
2. **Click on the upload area** or **drag and drop** your file
3. Accepted file types:
   - **Documents**: PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT
   - **Spreadsheets**: XLS, XLSX
   - **Presentations**: PPT, PPTX
   - **Images**: JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF
   - **Archives**: ZIP
4. Maximum file size: **10MB**
5. Once selected, you'll see the filename displayed

### Step 4: Submit
1. Click the **"Submit Assignment"** button (blue/purple gradient button)
2. Wait for the upload/submission to complete
3. You'll see a success message: "✅ Assignment submitted successfully!"
4. The page will refresh automatically

---

## ✅ After Submission

### What You'll See:
1. **Submission Confirmation**
   - Blue box showing "Your Submission"
   - Your submitted text (if any)
   - Download link for your uploaded file (if any)
   - Submission timestamp

2. **Download Your File**
   - Click **"Download Submitted File"** button to review what you uploaded

3. **Wait for Grading**
   - Status shows **"Submitted - Pending Review"**
   - Your teacher will review and grade your work

### When Your Assignment is Graded:
1. **Grade Badge** appears: **"Graded: XX/100"**
2. **Green box** shows your grade
3. **Teacher's Feedback** (if provided) appears below the grade

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Before Submitting:
- ✅ Check the **due date** - submissions after the due date are marked "Overdue"
- ✅ Verify your **file format** is accepted
- ✅ Ensure file size is **under 10MB**
- ✅ Double-check your work before submitting

### Submission Rules:
- ❌ **Cannot resubmit** - Once submitted, you cannot change it
- ✅ **Download your file** after submission to verify it's correct
- ✅ **Keep a backup** of your files before submitting

### File Naming Tips:
- Use descriptive names: `Assignment1_JohnDoe.pdf`
- Avoid special characters in filenames
- Include your name if required by instructor

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Problem: "Please enter your submission content or upload a file"
**Solution**: You need to either type something OR upload a file (or both)

### Problem: File won't upload
**Solutions**:
- Check file size (must be under 10MB)
- Verify file type is supported
- Try a different browser
- Check your internet connection

### Problem: "Assignment already submitted"
**Solution**: You've already submitted this assignment. Contact your teacher if you need to resubmit.

### Problem: Can't see the "Submit Assignment" button
**Solutions**:
- Check if you're enrolled in the course
- Check if the assignment is already submitted
- Verify you're logged in as a Student
